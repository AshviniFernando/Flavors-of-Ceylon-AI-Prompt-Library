# 01 — Business Context

## Business Profile

| Business Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| Business Name | Flavors of Ceylon |
| Industry | Food Manufacturing and Hospitality |
| Location | Australia |
| Market | Australian consumers |
| Product Price Range | AUD $7–$19 |
| Business Model | Production and distribution of authentic Sri Lankan food products |

## 1. Business Background

Flavors of Ceylon is an Australian-based food
production business concept that aims to introduce
authentic Sri Lankan cuisine to the Australian market.

The business focuses on convenient, affordable and
high-quality food products while preserving traditional
Sri Lankan recipes and culinary culture.

## 2. Product Range

### Savoury and Sweet Snacks

- Sri Lankan fish roti
- Vegetable rolls
- Halapa

### Frozen Meals and Ready-to-Eat Products

- Hot chicken kottu
- String hopper meal packs
- Rice and curry packets
- Egg hoppers and plain hoppers
- Onion sambal and fish curry

### Traditional Accompaniments

- Fresh, portion-controlled pol sambol sachets

## 3. Target Customers

The proposed customer segments include:

- Australian consumers interested in multicultural food
- Sri Lankan communities living in Australia
- Busy professionals and families
- Consumers seeking convenient meal options
- Potential retail and hospitality partners

## 4. Operational Challenges

| Business Function | Potential Challenge |
|---|---|
| Demand Planning | Difficulty estimating future product demand |
| Inventory | Ingredient shortages and avoidable food waste |
| Procurement | Repetitive supplier communication |
| Manufacturing | Inefficient production and batch planning |
| Quality Assurance | Inconsistent incident documentation |
| Product Quality | Incomplete ingredient and allergen information |
| Customer Service | Repetitive customer enquiries |
| Customer Experience | Delayed complaint analysis |
| Marketing | Time-consuming content preparation |
| Management | Manual business performance reporting |

These challenges are proposed for the business case
study and are not independently verified operational
findings.

## 5. Proposed AI Solution

The proposed solution is a library of 10 reusable
AI prompts supporting important business functions
within Flavors of Ceylon.

The prompt library will explore opportunities to
reduce repetitive work, improve information
consistency and support business decision-making.

## 6. Business Objectives

- Improve operational efficiency.
- Reduce repetitive administrative tasks.
- Support better ingredient and production planning.
- Reduce avoidable food waste.
- Improve customer communication.
- Support consistent marketing activities.
- Improve management decision support.
- Maintain responsible AI practices.

## 7. Human Oversight

AI-generated outputs will be treated as drafts or
decision-support information.

Production decisions, supplier commitments, product
labels, allergen information and food safety matters
must be reviewed by appropriate personnel.

AI must not independently certify food safety,
approve product release or guarantee allergen safety.

## 8. Project Scope

This project develops and evaluates AI prompts.
It does not implement a live production management
system or demonstrate actual commercial deployment.

All operational test data will be synthetic unless
clearly identified otherwise.
