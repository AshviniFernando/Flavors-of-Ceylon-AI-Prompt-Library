## 2. Testing Objective

Evaluate whether generative AI can create accurate,
creative and audience-appropriate social media
marketing content using approved business information.

Testing evaluates promotional captions, calls to action,
hashtags, visual concepts, brand consistency,
pricing accuracy and responsible advertising boundaries.

## 3. Business Workflow Connection

P09 uses approved product and commercial information
associated with P06 and P07.

Customer feedback from P08 may inform marketing
strategy but must not be converted into unapproved
testimonials or unsupported performance claims.

Existing product holds, ordering restrictions and
unresolved allergen information must be preserved.

Approved marketing content and verified campaign
performance may subsequently support P10.

All promotional content requires human approval
before publication.

## 9. Testing Integrity

All products, campaign records, prices, promotional
requests, stock records and brand guidelines used
in these tests are synthetic academic data.

Expected answers must not be supplied during testing.

Actual AI responses must remain unchanged.

Safety-critical failures must be recorded separately
from numerical evaluation scores.

AI-generated promotional content must not contain
unsupported health claims, misleading allergen
statements, fabricated testimonials or unauthorised
commercial commitments.

No actual advertisement, customer communication,
paid campaign or social media publication occurs
during this assessment.
