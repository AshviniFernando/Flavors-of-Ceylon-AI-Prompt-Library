# 05 — Evaluation and Business Impact

## Section Purpose

This section evaluates the performance, business
value, automation potential and limitations of
the Flavors of Ceylon AI prompt library.

## 1. Proposed Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Description |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Correct use of supplied information |
| Completeness | Coverage of required task elements |
| Consistency | Appropriate structure and output reliability |
| Business Relevance | Suitability for the intended workflow |
| Responsible AI | Appropriate handling of risks and uncertainty |

## 2. Proposed Scoring Method

Each criterion will be evaluated using:

- 0 = Not achieved
- 1 = Partially achieved
- 2 = Fully achieved

Maximum score: 10 points per evaluated output.

The scoring method is a project evaluation tool,
not the university's official marking rubric.

Any critical safety failure must be addressed
regardless of the numerical score.

## 3. Automation Potential

Prompts will be evaluated using three levels.

### High

AI can prepare most of the required draft output,
with appropriate human verification.

### Medium

AI can support the task, but substantial review
or additional business information is required.

### Low

The task requires significant professional judgement
or involves high-risk decisions.

## 4. Proposed Business Performance Indicators

| Indicator | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Task Completion Time | Manual time compared with AI-assisted time |
| Output Accuracy | Percentage of correctly handled information |
| Rework Requirement | Number of corrections required |
| Customer Response Efficiency | Time required to prepare a reviewed reply |
| Documentation Completeness | Required fields correctly completed |
| Risk Management | Identification and escalation of critical issues |

## 5. Risks and Limitations

The evaluation will consider:

- AI hallucination
- Incorrect calculations
- Incomplete business information
- Privacy and data confidentiality
- Bias and inconsistent outputs
- Over-reliance on AI
- Food safety and allergen risks
- Regulatory compliance limitations

## 6. Human-in-the-Loop Requirements

AI outputs must be reviewed by relevant employees
before operational use.

Food safety, product labels, allergen declarations,
supplier commitments and business-critical decisions
require appropriate human approval.

## 7. Business Impact

Potential operational and strategic benefits will
be evaluated using actual prompt testing results.

Any projected commercial benefits will be clearly
distinguished from measured testing outcomes.

## Evaluation Status

Pending prompt development and practical testing.
