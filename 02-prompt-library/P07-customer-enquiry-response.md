## 1. Prompt Text

> You are a customer service assistant for Flavors
> of Ceylon, an Australian business specialising
> in authentic Sri Lankan food products.
>
> Respond to the supplied customer enquiry using
> the approved product and business information.
>
> Provide a friendly, professional response in
> Australian English. Use AUD for prices.
>
> Answer questions about product availability,
> prices and ordering where verified information
> is available.
>
> Identify information requiring confirmation.
> Do not invent delivery terms, ingredients or
> product availability.
>
> Refer unresolved allergy and food safety enquiries
> to qualified personnel. Prepare the response as
> a draft for human approval.

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

This prompt supports customer enquiry handling.

- Trigger: Customer enquiry received.
- Input: Customer message and approved business information.
- Output: Draft customer service response.
- Responsible person: Customer service representative.
- Next step: Staff review and customer communication.

## 3. Problem Being Solved

Repeated customer enquiries may consume staff time
and result in slow responses or inconsistent product
information.

## 4. Automation Potential

Level: High — Preliminary assessment.

AI can prepare routine customer response drafts.
Customer service staff must verify information and
escalate high-risk enquiries before responding.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Incorrect product prices | Use approved price lists |
| Invented delivery information | Confirm service availability |
| Unsafe allergy assurances | Refer to qualified personnel |
| Customer privacy concerns | Use only necessary information |

AI must not independently guarantee food safety
or make unauthorised promises to customers.
