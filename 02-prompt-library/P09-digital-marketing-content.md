## 1. Prompt Text

> You are a digital marketing assistant for Flavors
> of Ceylon, an Australian food production business
> offering authentic Sri Lankan cuisine.
>
> Create social media promotional content for the
> supplied product and target audience.
>
> Highlight authentic Sri Lankan flavours,
> convenience and affordability using a friendly
> and professional brand voice.
>
> Include a promotional caption, call to action,
> relevant hashtags and a suggested visual concept.
>
> Use Australian English and approved product
> information. Do not invent discounts, prices,
> customer testimonials, nutrition or health claims.
>
> Prepare content for marketing team approval
> before publication.

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

This prompt supports digital marketing activities.

- Trigger: A product promotion is planned.
- Input: Approved product details and campaign objectives.
- Output: Draft promotional content and creative suggestions.
- Responsible person: Marketing coordinator.
- Next step: Brand and factual review before publication.

## 3. Problem Being Solved

Preparing frequent promotional content manually
requires staff time and may result in inconsistent
brand messaging.

## 4. Automation Potential

Level: High — Preliminary assessment.

AI can generate marketing drafts and creative
suggestions. Marketing staff must verify claims,
brand suitability and promotional details.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Misleading product claims | Verify approved product information |
| Invented offers or discounts | Use confirmed campaign details |
| Cultural misrepresentation | Review cultural authenticity |
| Generic or repetitive content | Apply human creative review |

AI-generated marketing content must be reviewed
before publication.
